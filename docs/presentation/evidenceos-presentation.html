04 / 14 — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE EPISTEMIC SEPARATION OF POWERS

Five-Pillar Verification Architecture

AI interprets. Rules decide. Uncertainty escalates. Evidence proves. Audit logs preserve.

Pillar 1 • Epistemic Multimodal Extraction Explicit Epistemic Typing (`FACT` | `INFERENCE` | `UNCERTAINTY`)

Every extracted claim carries an epistemic tag, confidence score, and exact source provenance (`evidence_id`, `sha256`, page/char offset, or pixel bounding grid). Blurry/dark images (`conf < 0.50`) and hedged audio (`conf = 0.58`) emit `UNCERTAINTY`, never fabricated facts.

Pillar 2 • Cross-Modal Entity & Conflict Graph SKU Grounding & Contradiction Isolation

Links claims across PDFs, photos, and audio into a directed Evidence DAG (`Shipment → Item → Claim → Evidence → Conflict`), surfacing quantity mismatches, chronology violations (`Challan < PO`), and uncorroborated single-source claims.

Pillar 3 • Cryptographic + Perceptual Fraud Detection SHA-256 + 64-bit Perceptual `dHash` (`Hamming <= 6`)

Cross-checks every incoming dock photo against the historical ledger. Catches both exact byte duplicates and recompressed, cropped, or brightness-shifted reused photos (`100.0%` recall on 40 test pairs).

Pillar 4 • Deterministic Rules (`R1–R8`) & Audit Chain Zero Hallucinated Arithmetic + SHA-256 Hash Chain

Enforces 48-hour SLA windows, high-value manual review thresholds (`>= $5,000`), and 2-source corroboration. Every event is chained (`previous_event_hash → event_hash`) in a tamper-evident ledger.
